# Sketch 003: Home, Warm (Redo)

## Design Question
005 was rejected as fussy and cold — brass everywhere, jittery pulsing door windows, an unclear "kickplate," boxed plaque labels, competing bunting/rug patterns, and an overall "1920s/funeral" mood instead of welcoming. This is a full rebuild, not a patch: a new theme (`home-v2.css`, brighter and warmer than `home.css`), and a much simpler composition.

## How to View
```
open .planning/sketches/home/003-home-warm/index.html
```

Only one version this round — the brief was "fix it," not "give me options." Click the glowing Ship door to open the portal; "↺ Reset to Home" closes it.

## What changed from home sketch 002
- **Candles**: real flame shape (teardrop gradient) on a plain candlestick, flickering only via opacity/scale — no more borrowed animation misapplied to door windows.
- **Doors**: flat painted colors (gold, teal, a muted locked tone), a plain doorknob, nothing else on the door face. No windows, no kickplate, no plaque box.
- **Door labels**: plain gold all-caps text directly on the background, no chip/box — as asked.
- **Ship door's glow**: a slow, smooth breathing shadow around the whole door (opacity only), not a pulsing circle.
- **Removed entirely**: bunting, the striped rug, portraits, wreaths — the multicolor-on-brown combination was reading as busy and somber rather than lively.
- **Background**: warmer and lighter overall, with a generous golden wash from above instead of a flat dark walnut.
- **Join card**: equal-width columns (`flex: 1` both sides), a much bigger QR (128px, up from 78px), and the divider is now two separate line segments with "or" sitting in the gap between them — no border/box around the text.

## What to Look For
- Does this read as "welcoming living room" rather than "dim saloon" now?
- Are three flat-colored doors with just a label enough, or does it now feel too bare after 005 was too busy — is there a middle ground?
- Is the Ship door's glow readable as "this one's alive" without looking buggy?
- Join card: does the bigger QR / equal columns / cleaner divider fix what you didn't like?
