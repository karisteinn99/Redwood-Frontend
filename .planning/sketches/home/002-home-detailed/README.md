# Sketch 002: Home, Detailed

## Design Question
Home sketch 001's Warm Hallway won, but the feedback was that it read as monotone brown despite `home.css` already defining coral/gold/sage accents — they just weren't being *used*. This round deliberately spends those colors on real decoration, redoes the join instructions as a two-column code-left/QR-right layout with a vertical "or" divider, and gives each door real detail instead of a plain rounded rectangle.

## How to View
```
open .planning/sketches/home/002-home-detailed/index.html
```

Click the glowing Ship door to open the portal (unchanged mechanic from home sketch 001); "↺ Reset to Home" closes it.

## Variants
- **A: Cozy Hallway** — bunting strung above the doors (coral/gold/sage flags), brass wall sconces flanking the welcome text, a woven rug underfoot, and doors with a round window, two panel insets, a brass kickplate, and an engraved brass plaque each. The Ship's window glows teal; the locked door's window is boarded over with planks.
- **B: Storyteller's Hallway** — everything in A, plus two framed "portraits" flanking the welcome (a placeholder for future recurring characters) and wreaths flanking the doors row.

Both use the new join card: **Join at gohomegames.com/join/JOLLY-7** on the left, a vertical line with **or** centered on it, **Scan** + a large QR on the right — as specified.

## What to Look For
- Does it read as "tavern/game-night" now, rather than "one shade of brown"?
- Do the door windows (glowing teal for the Ship, warm gold for the Lounge, boarded for the locked door) do useful work — can you tell something about each door before reading its plaque?
- A vs B: does B's extra decoration (portraits, wreaths) earn its place, or does it compete with the doors/join-card for attention?
- Is the join card's layout — code left, QR right, vertical "or" — actually easier to scan at a glance than the old stacked QR-dominant version?
