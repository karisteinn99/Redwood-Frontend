# Home Sketch 004: Cozy Cabin

## Design Question
Every previous Home attempt leaned on one dominant saturated brown, read as sepia/old-timey rather than welcoming. This is a new theme entirely (`home-cabin.css`, not a tweak of `home.css`/`home-v2.css`): a cooler, more neutral dark wood, a green garden element, and a violet "house magic" color used *only* on the portal door — not as the room's general accent.

## How to View
```
open .planning/sketches/home/004-home-cozy-cabin/index.html
```

Click the glowing Ship door — light leaks from underneath it and a few sparks drift near the frame, rather than the whole door pulsing. "↺ Reset to Home" closes it.

## What changed from home sketch 003
- **New palette** (`home-cabin.css`): cooler, more neutral wood tones instead of saturated orange — plus a subtle wood-grain texture overlay for realism.
- **Garden window**: a small arched window showing a moonlit night garden — bushes in two greens, a soft moon glow, three twinkling fireflies. This is the "green" and the first real piece of "outside the cabin."
- **Portal magic, reconsidered**: violet light leaking from under the Ship door plus a few drifting sparks near the frame — not a flat pulsing ring around a painted door. The violet is deliberately Home's own color, distinct from the Ship's own teal, so it reads as "magic belongs to the house," not "here's a preview of the room's colors."
- **Doors are wood again**, not flat saturated paint — a subtle vertical grain, a plain dark knob. Only the Ship door does anything unusual; the Lounge door is inert wood with just an ember-colored label (it isn't built yet), and the locked door stays dark and plain.
- Candles, the join card layout, and the gold all-caps door labels are carried over unchanged — none of that was the complaint.

## What to Look For
- Does this read as "cozy cabin" now, rather than sepia/tomb/tavern?
- Does the garden window actually register as "green/garden," or is it too small/subtle?
- Does the sparks-and-light-under-the-door treatment feel more genuinely magical than a glowing ring did — is it enough, or does the portal still need more?
- Is reserving violet strictly for "magic/portals" (vs. the Ship's teal, vs. Home's neutral wood) a rule worth keeping as more rooms get built?
