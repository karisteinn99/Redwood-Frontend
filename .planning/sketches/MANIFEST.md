# Sketch Manifest (index)

Sketches are organized per **room** (not per game — see the "rooms, not swappable themes" decision below), matching how `plans/` is split by area. `001` and `002` predate that split: they explored general direction before "rooms" existed as a concept, and both fed into two different rooms' identities, so they stay here as shared foundational history rather than being filed under one room.

## Decision: rooms, not swappable themes
Different rooms of Home get their own permanent aesthetic (e.g. "the Ship" for dice/bluffing games) rather than a single global app theme, and rather than a player-selectable skin system. Preserves the persistent-world/recurring-character premise from the original product brief; a player walks into a different-looking room, they don't reskin the app. Also recorded in `AGENTS.md` and `plans/home.md`.
